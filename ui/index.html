Attest

This is the bank's seat. The bank never touches the vendor's systems — it asks a control question, and the vendor's own agent runs the collector on its own infrastructure and hands back evidence. Two gates then decide whether that evidence supports the answer.

Pick a vendor and press Request attestation. Nothing is cached at the bank — the vendor's collectors run when you ask.
The severity counter is deterministic and offline. The relevance judge is a Runtype agent asked one question only: can this evidence answer this control at all? Neither overrides the other — when they split, the control escalates and nothing is recorded. Evidence is produced by the vendor, and its digest binds the bundle, not its truth.
